4. DESCRIPTION 
 
 
    The present invention discloses a computer -implemented grievance management system, hereinafter 
referred to as DistrictCare, developed to support smart district governance through a transparent, efficient, 
and participatory mechanism for handling public grievances. The system establishes a centralized digital 
environment that enables citizens to report civic issues associated with essential public service departments 
such as road infrastructure, electricity services, sewage or waste management, and water su pply. The 
invention ensures systematic routing of grievances to the appropriate governmental authorities, continuous 
monitoring through defined administrative procedures, and accountable resolution within stipulated 
timelines. 
    DistrictCare is realized as a web -based and cloud -enabled platform comprising a citizen interaction 
interface, a grievance handling and routing engine, a role -oriented administrative control framework, a 
geospatial mapping and visualization layer, a secure data management and analytics layer, and an artificial 
intelligence-driven assistance module. The system accommodates multiple categories of users including 
citizens, department -level administrators, and a district -level supervisory authority. Secure access to 
system functionalities is enforced through identity verification using multiple credentials such as username, 
password, mobile number, Aadhaar number, and electronic mail address, supplemented by one -time 
password verification for sensitive opera tions. Role -based access control restricts department 
administrators to grievances pertaining only to their respective departments, while granting the super 
administrator comprehensive visibility across all departments within the district. 
    Citizens interact with the platform through a structured grievance submission interface that facilitates 
selection of the relevant department, identification of the nature of the issue, and entry of a detailed 
complaint description. Each grievance is linke d to a geographically tagged location obtained through an 
interactive digital map that captures latitude and longitude coordinates within a predefined district 
boundary. The system permits submission of image -based proof to substantiate reported issues  and 
optionally supports attachment of written representations from community members or voice -based 
explanations to enhance accessibility and clarity. To minimize redundancy and reduce administrative 
burden, a spatial validation mechanism is employed to p revent acceptance of duplicate or substantially 
similar grievances within a predefined geographic radius. 
    Upon submission, grievances are displayed on a public -facing feed integrated with a map -based 
visualization interface. Personal identification details of complainants are concealed from public view to 
preserve privacy while maintaining transparency of comp laint information. Each grievance is visually 
represented with indicators denoting its current processing state, including pending, under action, resolved, 
or rejected. Community users are enabled to interact with grievances through support mechanisms such as 
upvoting, which assist in conveying the relative urgency and severity of reported issues. Authorized 
administrative personnel communicate verification progress and corrective measures through official 
comments that are accessible to the public.

    Department administrators are empowered to validate grievance authenticity, update processing status, 
assign field -level officials based on geographic relevance, and document remedial actions undertaken. 
Automated notifications are generated to inform citi zens of any changes in grievance status. A grievance 
is designated as resolved only after confirmation from the complainant, supported by uploaded evidence 
demonstrating that the reported issue has been adequately addressed. Complaints identified as fa lse, 
unverifiable, or non-compliant with system policies are rejected and excluded from subsequent processing 
and analysis. 
    The invention further incorporates an artificial intelligence -based assistance module configured to 
support users by categorizing grievances, recommending the appropriate department, providing 
departmental office details and contact information, and explai ning relevant laws, rules, and regulations 
governing civic services. In addition, the system generates analytical insights for administrative users by 
aggregating grievance data to present department -wise statistics, resolution trends, and performance 
indicators. These analytics facilitate informed decision -making and strengthen accountability in district -
level governance. 
Accordingly, DistrictCare provides a scalable, secure, and intelligent digital framework that modernizes 
public grievance redressal by integrating geolocation validation, community participation, structured 
administrative workflows, and artificial intellig ence within a unified platform suitable for smart district 
governance.

CLAIMS 
 
I/We Claim, 
1. A computer-implemented grievance redressal system for smart district governance, 
comprising: 
a citizen interface configured to receive civic grievances; 
a secure role-based authentication and authorization module; 
a grievance processing and departmental routing module; 
a geolocation verification and duplicate grievance prevention module; and 
an administrative workflow management module; 
wherein the system enables transparent, location-verified, and department-specific handling and 
resolution of civic grievances within a district. 
2. The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein the civic grievances relate to one or more public 
service departments including road infrastructure, electricity services, sewage or waste 
management, and water supply services. 
3. The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein the authentication and authorization module verifies 
users using multiple identifiers including username, password, mobile number, Aadhaar 
number, and electronic mail address, and assigns role-specific access permissions. 
4. The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein the system supports multiple user roles comprising 
citizen users, department administrators, and a district-level supervisory administrator, each role 
having predefined access privileges and operational responsibilities. 
5. The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein the citizen interface is configured to receive 
structured grievance information including department category, issue type, descriptive content, 
and supporting evidence comprising image files or voice recordings. 
6. The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein the geolocation verification and duplicate grievance 
prevention module associates each grievance with geographic coordinates restricted to a 
predefined district boundary and restricts submission of substantially similar grievances within 
a predetermined geographic radius. 
7. The system as claimed in claim 1, wherein the grievance processing and departmental routing 
module assigns each grievance to a corresponding department administrator based on the 
grievance category and geographic location.

ABSTRACT OF THE INVENTION 
 
 
    The present invention pertains to a computer -based grievance redressal platform intended for 
implementation in smart district governance systems. The platform is developed to promote active 
citizen participation, operational transparency, and improved effectiveness in the delivery of public 
services. The system, identified as DistrictCare, provides a secure digital environment through which 
citizens can submit complaints associated with essential civic services including road maintenance, 
electrical supply, sewage or solid waste management, and water distribution. Each submitted grievance 
undergoes identity verification, geographic location tagging, and attachment of supporting proof to 
ensure validity and to restrict repeated submissions within a specified spatial range. 
    The system operates through a hierarchical administrative structure that includes citizen users, 
department-level administrative officers, and a district -level supervisory authority, thereby enabling 
organized complaint assignment and resolution. The platf orm offers real -time tracking of grievance 
status, public display of complaint progress with complainant anonymity, and community participation 
features to indicate the urgency and seriousness of reported issues. An integrated artificial intelligence 
module assists users by categorizing complaints, identifying the appropriate department, and providing 
access to applicable regulations and departmental contact information. Additionally, the system 
incorporates an interactive map interface and analytical dashboards to support data-driven governance 
decisions. Grievance closure is completed only after confirmation by the complainant, thereby 
strengthening accountability, reducing response delays, and facilitating transparent, efficient, and 
participatory governance at the district level.
